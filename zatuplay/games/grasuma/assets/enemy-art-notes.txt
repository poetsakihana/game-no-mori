制作版 敵キャラ画像について

通常敵12種とボス4種。原作の全敵一覧・正確な公式デザインではなく、グラフィティスマッシュ風の創作デザインです。
画像生成方法：内蔵の画像生成ツール。既存のタイトル・ミニキャラに合う、輪郭のはっきりしたモバイルRPG風イラストを使用。
属性・分類は制作版の表示用。原作の種族と個別性能の確定資料ではありません。

初回生成プロンプト：

Primary request: richly illustrated colorful chibi enemy monsters for a Japanese pull-and-fling graffiti battle fantasy RPG, 2017-era mobile JRPG enemy art aesthetic, chunky clean silhouettes, dark ink outlines, cel shaded detailed painterly highlights, fantasy creatures tainted with crystalline arc energy, readable at tiny sizes. Not portraits: FULL BODY sprites, three-quarter front view, facing slightly toward viewer. Every sprite separately centered inside its equal-sized cell, generous transparent margins at all cell edges (at least 12%), no overlap between cells, no borders, no shadows beyond cells, NO TEXT, no logos, no UI. 2048 square if possible. Precise row-major order left to right: ROW 1: 1 small red orange horned furry wild boar with dark rock armor; 2 blue armored crab with huge claws and bright cyan core; 3 purple bat with broad wings and gold horns; 4 emerald carnivorous flower plant monster with leaf limbs. ROW 4 BOSSES all imposing powerful full bodies: 13 giant armored dark lion chimera with purple crystal back and flaming mane; 14 enormous orange crimson raven phoenix monster with obsidian beak, spread wings; 15 ancient giant bronze fortress guardian automaton with blue energy cannon arms; 16 menacing floating ornate mechanical violet energy nucleus with six segmented metallic claw arms arranged radially. Consistent visual finish and size within each cell. Single transparent sprite sheet, no background, no floor, no writing. Original designs intended as fan game assets, not a claim of exact original monster replication.

最終調整指示：16体のデザインと並び順を保ち、透明背景の正確な4×4等分グリッドに再配置。各セルの中央72%以内に全身・翼・炎・爪を収め、全方向に14%以上の透明な余白を確保。切り抜きや隣接セルへのはみ出しを避ける。文字・罫線なし。
